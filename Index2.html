Key takeaways

Strength

City-hub network is stable and near target

KHI Proper delivered 820K+ of the quarter's volume at ~93.7% achievement, with DHA, Express and Malir hubs all above 95% in June. The core engine is healthy.

Priority

Branch network is dragging and worsening

Balochistan coastal branches fell from 64% → 63% → 54% achievement across the quarter, with 49% of branch shipments stuck 2+ days before assignment. Small volume, outsized reputational risk.

Watch

June softening vs May

Zone SR slipped from 77.2% (May) to 75.7% (June) and achievement from 93.2% to 92.8%. LHK hub (79% ach) and rising 2+ day critical assignments are the main causes.

Opportunity

Speed is the single biggest lever

Shipments assigned within 3 days deliver at 76% vs 58% once aged past a week — an 18-point swing. Tightening same-day assignment directly lifts SR toward target.

3 · 5-Whys — drilling the #1 driver

“Consignee not available” · 48.7% of failures

Why do so many first attempts fail?

WHY 1
Deliveries fail because the consignee is not available when the rider arrives (48.7% of RTAs).
WHY 2
Because there is no advance coordination on delivery timing — the customer isn’t told when to expect the rider.
WHY 3
Because riders don’t pre-call / WhatsApp-confirm before attempting, and no time-slot is captured at booking.
WHY 4
Because the app workflow doesn’t enforce a pre-attempt contact step, and incentives reward attempts, not confirmed deliveries.
ROOT
No enforced pre-delivery coordination process (call/robo-WhatsApp + time-slot) tied to a rider KPI. Same root drives the 20% “refused / not available” and address-clarification failures.

Two supporting root-cause chains

ECOM
Ecom delivers at 61.6% vs Express 90.6%. → low app adoption (51%) → returns/COD friction → no field discipline enforced → root: Ecom rider process not app-governed.
ALLIED
Branch network at 57% achievement. → 49% of branch shipments wait 2+ days to assign → thin coastal capacity + linehaul delay → root: no minimum service model for remote coastal branches.
COMMON
Both trace back to process not being standardised & enforced through the app — the same lever as the #1 driver.

4 · Way Forward — PDCA countermeasures

Plan → Do → Check → Act · owners & targets for Q3
Plan

Target the vital few

Set Q3 goal: zone SR 76.4% → 80%+. Prioritise the 3 Pareto reasons & the Allied network. Define one owner per root cause.

Do

Deploy countermeasures

Enforce pre-delivery robo-call/WhatsApp + time-slot; mandate app assignment for Ecom; branch turnaround SOP; address-quality gate at booking.

Check

Measure weekly

Track first-attempt %, consignee-not-available rate, Ecom app adoption, branch achievement & RC-to-arrival on a weekly control chart.

Act

Standardise & scale

Lock wins into SOP & rider KPIs; roll the winning playbook from best hubs (DHA/Express) to LHK & the coastal branches.
